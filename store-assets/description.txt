Bring a little greenery to your wrist.

Monstera pairs bold pixel numerals with richly detailed forest-green leaves and a soft, warm gray-ivory background. Designed specifically for Pebble Time 2, it keeps the time clear while giving your watch a calm botanical look.

FEATURES
• Large digital clock with your preferred 12- or 24-hour format
• Weekday and date
• Battery gauge and percentage
• Temperature in Fahrenheit or Celsius with weather icons
• Daily steps and the latest available heart-rate reading

SETTINGS
Choose °F or °C and independently show or hide weather, steps, battery, and heart rate in the Pebble app. Your preferences are saved.

Weather is provided by Open-Meteo and updates through your phone every 30 minutes. It requires location permission and an internet connection. No API key or separate companion app is needed.

Health readings depend on device support and available Pebble Health data. Unavailable readings appear as dashes. Time, battery, artwork, and available health readings work offline.

Made for Pebble Time 2 (200 × 228). The warm background uses fine dithering to suit the watch’s color palette.
